📖 HOW TO PLAY CHAOS ARENA

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Score the highest points in 90 seconds! Race around the arena collecting coins, snagging power-ups, bouncing off bumpers, and outliving sudden Chaos Events!

🕹️ CONTROLS

  • Desktop / Laptop: Use W A S D or Arrow Keys to move smoothly.
  • Mobile / Tablet: Use the intuitive on-screen touch virtual joystick on the left.

⚡ POWER-UPS

⚡ Speed Boost: +60% turbo speed for 7 seconds.
🛡️ Shield: Blocks 1 hazard hit or chaos penalty safely.
🧲 Coin Magnet: Attracts coins within 240px for 8 seconds.
⭐ Double Score: Every coin collected gives 2X points for 10s!

🌀 SIGNATURE CHAOS EVENTS (EVERY 20 SECONDS)

Every 20 seconds, the arena destabilizes with an authoritative 10-second chaos modifier:

  • Slippery Floor: Floor turns to frictionless ice! Huge momentum drift.
  • Reverse Controls: Directional inputs are inverted!
  • Coin Rush: Golden bonus coins flood the arena floor!
  • Giant Coins: Coins transform into 50-point mega coins!
  • Overdrive Hazards: Laser drones move at double patrol speed!
  • Shrinking Zone: Arena safe perimeter shrinks; stay inside or lose points!
  • Speed Frenzy: Nitro boost given to ALL players simultaneously!

🛡️ HAZARDS & BUMPERS

Avoid laser patrol drones (causes knockback, stun, and loss of 5 points unless shielded!). Use spring pinball bumpers for tactical ricochets!
